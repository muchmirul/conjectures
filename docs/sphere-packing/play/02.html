2 · Add dimensions and watch the room go

One ball, and the box that just holds it. The slider changes the dimension.

Try this. Take the dimension up to 20. The ball keeps its width the whole way, and the fraction of the box it fills falls off a cliff.

The ball's own volume is largest in dimension five, which you can see by reading the volume line as you move the slider.
