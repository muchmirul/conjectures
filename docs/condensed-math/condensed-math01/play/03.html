3 · Land a probe without tearing it

The approaching probe on the left: points marching towards a limit. A target line on the right. You choose where the marching points head, and where the limit point lands.

Try this. Set the two sliders apart and the chain snaps at the limit: the landing is refused. Bring them together and it is accepted. That gap, and nothing else, is what continuity means to a probe.

Every space you can measure distance in is determined by which sequences converge, so this one probe already reads all of them. That is Remark 1.6 of the lectures.
